Your privacy matters

AMEXA respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information while providing local shopping and delivery services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use AMEXA, we may collect information required to provide our services.

  • Name and mobile number
  • Delivery address
  • Location information
  • Order details
  • Payment-related information
  • Customer support information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve AMEXA services, including account login, nearby store discovery, order processing, delivery, customer support and service improvements.

3. Location Information

AMEXA may request your location to show nearby stores, confirm delivery availability and help delivery partners deliver orders to the correct location.

4. Orders and Payments

Information related to orders and payments may be stored as necessary for order processing, customer support, refunds, accounting and fraud prevention.

5. Sharing of Information

AMEXA may share limited information with shops, delivery partners and service providers only when required to complete an order or provide AMEXA services.

We do not intend to sell customers' personal information for unrelated marketing purposes.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect customer information. However, no internet-based system can guarantee complete security. Customers should also protect their account and mobile device.

7. OTP and Account Access

AMEXA may use OTP verification for account access. Never share your OTP with anyone claiming to be from AMEXA.

8. Customer Choices

Customers may contact AMEXA support regarding their account, personal information, delivery address or privacy-related concerns.

9. Changes to This Policy

AMEXA may update this Privacy Policy as the platform, services or legal requirements change.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact AMEXA through the Help & Support section.
